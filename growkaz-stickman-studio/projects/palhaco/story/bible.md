# Palhaço — story bible
**Logline:** At a backyard birthday party, a strange clown jumps the wall and plays a "game" with the kids. He is really an undercover agent, and the boy's biggest present is a bomb from Wallace's dad.
**Tone:** party, then suspense, then thriller, then relief. Narrated in the first person by the birthday boy ("eu").
